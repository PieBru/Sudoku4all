What you will learn

You know the rules of Sudoku. This chapter gives you four little tricks. Each trick finds one number. Then you write the number in. These tricks carry you through whole easy boards. Champions use them too. They are just faster.

Trick 1: Cross-hatching

Pick a number. Let us pick 5. Find every 5 on the board. Now choose one box. A row with a 5 is blocked. A column with a 5 is blocked too. Blocked lines cross out squares inside the box. Sometimes just one square stays free. That square gets the 5.

Grown-ups: cross-hatching scans the rows and columns that already contain a digit; their intersections block cells in a box until a single placement remains. It is the by-hand way to find a hidden single (SudokuWiki / HoDoKu, Brief Tier 1).

Trick 2: Full house

Look at one row. Eight numbers are already there. One number is missing. You know it instantly. Write it in. This works for a column. It works for a box too.

Grown-ups: a full house is any unit — row, column, or box — with a single empty cell; the missing digit is forced. It is the friendly corner case of the single family (Brief Tier 1).

Trick 3: Hidden single

Pick a number again. Say 7. Look at one box. Where can the 7 go? Rows and columns with a 7 say no. If one square says yes, the 7 lives there. It was hiding. You found it.

Grown-ups: a hidden single is a digit restricted to exactly one cell of a unit — a candidate that appears once. Cross-hatching is the scanning method; this is the same find, seen from the digit (Brief Tier 1).

Trick 4: Naked single

Now look at one empty square. Ask: which numbers fit here? The row already holds eight numbers. So only one number fits. That number goes in.

Grown-ups: a naked single is a cell whose candidate list holds exactly one digit. Here the row alone fixes it; rows, columns and boxes can each do the job (Brief Tier 1).

Practice on real boards

Now try real boards. These come from the game itself. Each has one solution. Find all the 7s first.

Grown-ups: both positions come straight from the game engine and are gate-verified to have exactly one solution — safe to reason over with the four singles tricks.

Try it in the game

Time to play for real. Open the game. Choose Easy. Press New game. Your first hunt: find every 7. Use cross-hatching on each box. Then hunt full houses.

Checkpoint

Can you do these three things?

  1. Find a hiding 5 inside a box.
  2. Fill a row that misses one number.
  3. Say which trick you just used.

You are ready when all three feel easy. Your goal: solve one easy board by yourself.
