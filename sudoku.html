How to play & keyboard

Fill the grid so that every row, every column, and each of the nine 3×3 boxes contains the digits 1–9 exactly once (classic Sudoku). Each puzzle has a unique solution.

Tap a cell, then tap a digit — or move with the ←↑↓→ keys and type 1–9.
N pencil notes (or hold Shift + digit) · E/Backspace erase · U or Ctrl+Z undo · H hint · P pause.

Your best time is saved per difficulty. The theme button cycles System → Light → Dark.
